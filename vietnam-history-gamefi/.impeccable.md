## Design Context

### Users
Players of Hào Khí Đại Việt who earn HKDV through gameplay and want to exchange it with SOL using their own Solana wallet. The DEX is reached from the game lobby and should work on desktop and mobile.

### Brand Personality
Historical Vietnamese, confident, and clear. The trading experience should feel like the game's Khu Giao Thương while giving players precise information before they sign a transaction.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use the existing lacquer red, antique gold, and dark surfaces of the game. Keep its existing Vietnamese-friendly typography and make the swap form the visual focus. Raydium's swap information hierarchy is a reference, while the final page remains part of the game's own identity.

### Design Principles
- Show live balances, quotes, fees, slippage, minimum received, and transaction state before and after wallet signing.
- Keep HKDV/SOL Devnet behavior and the existing authenticated wallet and backend flow intact.
- Make the primary swap path obvious; reveal operational details without overwhelming new players.
- Adapt the same complete trading flow to narrow screens with comfortable touch targets.
- Never imply a simulated value or transaction is live; label Devnet and unfinished modules clearly.
